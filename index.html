01
Is my business data safe and confidential?
+

Absolutely. Your data is yours and yours alone. SALIYO AI only accesses information you deliberately provide — nothing is pulled from your systems without your knowledge or approval. We never share, sell, or expose your business data to any third party. Sensitive information like contracts and financial details are never fed into the system — only operational data relevant to the automations you approve.

02
Do my employees need to learn new software?
+

No. Your employees never touch SALIYO AI directly. They continue using the tools they already know — Google Sheets and simple forms. If they can use WhatsApp, they can work with our system. SALIYO AI runs silently in the background. The only people who interact with the app directly are you and your designated system manager.

03
How much does it cost?
+

Every business is different — so our pricing is tailored to your size, industry, and the scope of automation you need. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all pricing. Get in touch and we'll put together a custom quote that fits your business perfectly.

04
What if the system makes a mistake?
+

Nothing sends without your approval. Every critical output — proposals, client emails, reports — goes through a human review step before it leaves the system. SALIYO AI drafts, you decide. This means the speed of AI with the judgment of a human. You are always the final authority on what goes out under your company's name.

05
How long before we see real results?
+

Within the first 30 days you will see faster proposal turnaround and automated client reports going out. Within 60 days the inquiry management and follow-up systems will be fully active. By month three, the efficiency gains will be clearly visible and measurable. Most clients notice a difference within the first week of going live.
